Deploy Chroma or VecturaDB in your own cloud

Open-source templates that create the server in your own cloud account, with HTTPS. Choose one, select Deploy to Azure or Launch Stack, and connect your Chroma client to the address it gives you.

Why your own cloud

  • Your account, your data

    The server, its disk, volume or share, and the backups are resources of your Azure subscription or of your AWS account.

  • Your cloud's prices, no fee from us

    Azure or AWS bills you at its list prices. The templates are free, under the Apache License 2.0.

  • Read it before you run it

    The templates, their scripts and the numbers we measured are in the VecturaDB.Deploy repository.

The numbers on these pages are what we measured between 25 and 29 October 2026, in westeurope on Azure and in eu-west-1 on AWS: your region, sizes, data and versions may behave differently. Not sure about your case? Open an issue in the VecturaDB repository, describe your data and your setup, and we will take a look on a best-effort basis.
