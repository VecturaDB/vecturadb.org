Chroma clients, tested on VecturaDB

In our tests, Chroma clients work with VecturaDB as they do with Chroma 1.5.9: we ran 15 of them, in 11 languages, and each gave the same result on both servers, step by step.

Results by client

  • Works: all 9 steps gave the expected result.
  • Partial: the steps the client has passed; the others are not in the client.
  • Fails: at least one step gave an error.

Each failure on this page happened in the same way on Chroma 1.5.9. Four of the five failing clients call the v1 API, which Chroma 1.x no longer serves.

In the details: the steps of each client, with its errors and notes; how we tested; and sparse vectors, BM25, the Search API and API keys, tested with the two Rust crates.
